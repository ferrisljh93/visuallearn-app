# VisualLearn — Testing-Evidence Addendum: Two Evidence Classes

**Date:** 24 Nov 2026
**Status:** Addendum to the pre-registered moderated learner-testing protocol. This document records the separation rules only — it amends no criterion, wording, or threshold.
**Governing documents:** `validation-test-pack-2026-11-23.md` (screening §1.1, session protocol §2, feedback instrument §3, offer §4.2 + thresholds §4.4, results template §5) and `session-testing-pack-2026-11-24.md` (the standardised 40-minute protocol, including §5 on self-guided testing).
**Sessions to date:** 0.

## Background

Until now VisualLearn has been hosted as a private Muse Space, so testing could only be owner-hosted: the owner runs the app and the learner works on the owner's screen. An independently deployable external MVP is now being built so learners can use the app directly on their own devices with their own accounts. That creates a second, materially different way of producing testing evidence. This addendum defines the two classes so their results are never confused.

## Class A — Owner-operated ASSISTED sessions

**Definition:** The existing moderated 40-minute protocol in `session-testing-pack-2026-11-24.md`. The owner hosts and operates/hosts the environment; the learner works on the owner's screen, under the facilitator script, observer sheet, feedback instrument (§3), and offer step (§4.2) exactly as written.

**What Class A CAN evidence:**
- Guided task completion under the protocol — T1–T5 completions, times, errors, help given (help = Partial), and the §5.2 task-completion rules.
- Comprehension with facilitator present — including the T4 prediction-before-use check and the T5 weakness-match judgement, both of which require an observer.
- Price confirmation under the pre-registered offer wording — signal (a): founding-list join with a real email AND the S$79/year price confirmed in the same step, collected in Segment 6 after the full, verified product experience.

**What Class A CANNOT evidence:**
- Independent discoverability — the learner did not find, open, or set up the product alone.
- Unaided onboarding — no account creation, first launch, or self-directed start on the learner's own device was tested.
- Real-world retention on own device — a hosted 40-minute session says nothing about whether the learner returns, unprompted, on their own device.

## Class B — DIRECT learner-operated usability testing

**Definition:** The learner uses the external MVP independently on their own device with their own account, unmoderated or lightly observed. No facilitator runs the session, gives the task instructions live, or applies the standard probes in the moment.

**What Class B CAN evidence:**
- Unaided onboarding — account creation, first launch, and getting started without the owner present.
- Independent task completion — whether the learner can complete tasks alone, on their own device, in their own environment.
- Drop-off points — where learners abandon, stall, or fail to proceed when nobody is there to help or observe.

**What Class B CANNOT evidence:**
- Moderated depth feedback — no live probing, think-aloud capture, verbatim error record, help/Partial grading, prediction-before-use verification, or weakness-match judgement of the kind the moderated protocol produces.
- Price confirmation — unless it is separately collected under the same pre-registered offer wording (§4.2) and founding-list confirmation step. A Class B join collected any other way is not signal (a).

Self-guided testing (see §5 of the session-testing pack) sits inside Class B and keeps its supplementary status: product-learning evidence only — task friction, feedback scores, Q7 missing-feature and Q11 first-fix answers — reported in its own bucket, labelled self-guided.

## Separation rules (mandatory)

1. **The two classes are reported separately and NEVER pooled into a single count or rate.** Every report, dashboard, and results log keeps Class A and Class B in separate buckets from the first record, with the (a)/(b)/(c) signal breakdown kept apart for each.
2. **The pre-registered thresholds apply to Class A ONLY:** the target of 10 independent moderated learner sessions, and the price-confirmation threshold of ≥4/10 price-confirmed = SIGNAL, 2–3 = INCONCLUSIVE, <2 = FAIL (§4.4).
3. **Class B results cannot be counted toward the 10-session target or the ≥4/10 threshold** — not Class B task completions, not Class B feedback, and not Class B founding-list joins, however they were collected.
4. **Any change to these criteria requires the owner's written amendment BEFORE any results in the affected class are examined.** No retroactive reclassification, no merging of buckets after the fact.
5. **Self-guided testing sits inside Class B and keeps its supplementary status** (per §5.5 of the session-testing pack). Self-guided sessions do not count toward the 10-session moderated target, and self-guided founding-list joins do not count toward the ≥4/10 signal-(a) threshold.
6. **This document amends no criterion.** It records the separation rules only. All screening criteria, task instructions, wording, the feedback instrument, the offer, and the thresholds in the governing documents are unchanged.

## Owner amendment — 24 Nov 2026 (later): Class A facilitation clarified

**Recorded:** 24 Nov 2026, by written owner ruling, **before any session in either class has run and before any results have been examined** — satisfying separation rule 4 above. **Sessions to date remain 0.**

**The clarification:** Class A sessions may be operated by the owner **or by an explicitly owner-authorised facilitator** acting under the owner's written arrangement. Class A is therefore: *owner-operated or explicitly owner-authorised facilitator-operated moderated sessions*. This aligns this addendum's Class A definition with the session testing pack's hosting rule ("sessions are hosted by Ferris or his explicit arrangement only"), which already contemplated the arranged-delegate case.

**What this amendment does NOT change:**
- The original task execution requirements are preserved in full: the standardised 40-minute protocol, the verbatim facilitator script and fixed probe set, the observer sheet, the frozen feedback instrument (§3), and the §4.2 offer step exactly as worded.
- The acceptance criteria are unchanged: the 10-session moderated target and the ≥4/10 price-confirmed threshold (§4.4) apply to Class A exactly as before, whoever operates the session.
- The Class A / Class B separation is unchanged: facilitator-operated moderated sessions under this amendment are Class A (they are fully moderated under the protocol); Class B remains direct learner-operated testing and still cannot count toward the Class A target or threshold. No pooling is introduced.
- Facilitator identity is recorded per session in the results log, so every Class A result is attributable to its operator.

**Supporting documents:** `facilitator-investigation-2026-11-24.md` (the analysis behind this ruling) and `facilitator-pack/` (guide, training plan, calibration assessment, consent documentation, fidelity undertaking, evidence-audit process). No facilitator has been engaged, contacted or shortlisted under this amendment; engaging a named person remains a separate owner decision.
